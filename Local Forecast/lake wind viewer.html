Source: MeteoSwiss – local forecasts from MeteoSwiss Open Data, licensed under CC BY 4.0 (terms of use). Values per lake are aggregated by this tool from the forecast points near the lake (see method above the map). Lake regions: Lake Constance West / Mitte / Ost as in the Bodensee storm warning service; Lake Zurich (Zürichsee / Obersee), Lake Lucerne (Alpnachersee, West / Ost divided at Unter Nas–Ober Nas, Urnersee) and Lake Geneva (Petit Lac / Grand Lac / Haut Lac) along the lake basins.

Map: © swisstopo (terms of use) · lake outlines: FSO generalised boundaries based on swisstopo data, via swiss-maps (BSD-3-Clause); Pfäffikersee (below 5 km², not in that dataset): outline digitised on map.geo.admin.ch (© swisstopo). Lakes and warning regions as on the MeteoSwiss lake warning map.

Not an official MeteoSwiss product; MeteoSwiss does not endorse this tool. Not a warning service: the gust markers and lake colours (≥ 25 kn / > 33 kn for more than half of a lake's forecast points) only show forecast values. The official warnings from MeteoSwiss are published on MeteoSwiss natural hazards – lakes. No guarantee is given for the correctness, accuracy, timeliness or completeness of the data or their presentation.
