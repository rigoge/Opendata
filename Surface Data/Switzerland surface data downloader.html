1 · Station network (category)
2 · Period (file version)
historical: from the start of the series to the end of last year · recent: 1 January of this year to yesterday · now: yesterday up to the latest value.
3 · Temporal resolution
Monthly (m) and yearly (y) files exist as a single file per station and are independent of the period selection. Only resolutions that exist in the chosen network are offered (read from the STAC assets).
3b · 10-year files (10 min and hourly, historical)
10-minute and hourly historical data are split into 10-year files, e.g. ogd-smn_bas_h_historical_2020-2029.csv. Select the decades you need; 10-minute files start in 2000 (2000-2009 onward), hourly files earlier; decades that do not exist for a resolution are skipped automatically. daily, monthly and yearly files are not affected.
